# Chronological Timeline of Technologies

This timeline presents key technological developments in chronological order, from the earliest stone tools to the Bronze Age.

## Lower Paleolithic (~3.3 Ma – 300 ka)

### ~3.3 Ma
- **Lomekwi Stone Tools** (Kenya) - Earliest known stone tool production
  - [[Lithic_Technologies/Paleolithic_Stone_Tools]]

### ~2.6 Ma – 1.7 Ma
- **Oldowan Industry** - First widespread stone tool tradition
  - Simple choppers and flakes
  - [[Lithic_Technologies/Choppers]]
  - [[Lithic_Technologies/Choppers-Regional]]
  - [[Lithic_Technologies/Stone-Tools]]
  - [[Lithic_Technologies/Hammerstone]]

### ~1.7 Ma – 300 ka
- **Acheulean Industry** - Handaxes and cleavers
  - Bifacial flaking, symmetrical tools
  - [[Lithic_Technologies/Axe]]
  - [[Lithic_Technologies/Choppers-Regional]]
  - [[Lithic_Technologies/Stone-Tools]]

## Middle Paleolithic (~300 ka – 50 ka)

### ~300 ka – 50 ka
- **Levallois Technique** - Prepared core technology
  - Associated with Neanderthals and early *Homo sapiens*
  - [[Lithic_Technologies/Paleolithic_Stone_Tools]]
  - [[Lithic_Technologies/Stone-Tools]]

### ~200 ka
- **Middle Stone Age (Africa)** - Regional innovations
  - Blade technology, ochre use
  - [[Lithic_Technologies/Paleolithic_Stone_Tools]]

## Upper Paleolithic (~50 ka – 12 ka)

### ~50 ka – 12 ka
- **Upper Paleolithic Revolution** - Behavioral modernity
  - Blade/bladelet technology, bone/antler tools, art
  - [[Lithic_Technologies/Paleolithic_Stone_Tools]]
  - [[Lithic_Technologies/Spearhead]]

### ~40 ka
- **Spear Thrower (Atlatl)** - Projectile enhancement
  - [[Lithic_Technologies/Spearhead]]
  - [[Lithic_Technologies/Stone-Tools]]

### ~30 ka
- **Eyed Needles** - Tailored clothing
  - [[CLOTHING/Fursuits]]
  - [[CLOTHING/Woven-Textiles]]

### ~25 ka
- **Natural Dyeing** - Ochre, plant pigments for textiles/body
  - [[CLOTHING/Dyeing-Techniques]]
  - [[CLOTHING/Bark-Cloth]]

### ~28 ka
- **Ceramic Figurines** (Dolní Věstonice) - Earliest ceramics
  - [[Lithic_Technologies/Neolithic_Pottery]]

### ~20 ka
- **Solutrean Pressure Flaking** - Thin bifaces
  - [[Lithic_Technologies/Stone-Tools]]
  - [[Lithic_Technologies/Spearhead]]

### ~40,000 years ago

## Mesolithic / Epipaleolithic (~12 ka – 8 ka)

### ~12 ka – 8 ka
- **Microlith Technology** - Composite tools
  - Geometric microliths for arrows/sickles
  - [[Lithic_Technologies/Stone-Tools]]
  - [[Lithic_Technologies/Spearhead]]

### ~11 ka
- **Earliest Pottery** (East Asia) - Jōmon, Xianrendong
  - [[Lithic_Technologies/Neolithic_Pottery]]
  - [[Asia/Neolithic-Technology]]

## Early Neolithic (~10 ka – 7 ka)

### ~10,000 BCE
- **Pre-Pottery Neolithic A (PPNA)** - Near East
  - Early cultivation, round houses
  - [[AGRICULTURE/Early-Agriculture-and-Domestication]]
  - [[AGRICULTURE/Crop-Domestication]]

### ~9,500 BCE
- **Göbekli Tepe** - Monumental architecture pre-agriculture
  - [[REGIONAL-MESOAMERICAN/Mesoamerican-Tech]] (comparative)
  - [[Europe/Neolithic-Technology]]

### ~9,000 BCE
- **Earliest Agriculture** - Fertile Crescent
  - Einkorn, emmer, barley, legumes
  - [[AGRICULTURE/Early-Agriculture-and-Domestication]]
  - [[AGRICULTURE/Crop-Domestication]]

### ~8,500 BCE
- **Pre-Pottery Neolithic B (PPNB)** - Rectangular houses, plaster
  - [[Europe/Neolithic-Technology]]
  - [[Asia/Neolithic-Technology]]

### ~8,000 BCE
- **Rice Cultivation Begins** - Yangtze Valley
  - [[Asia/Neolithic-Technology]]
  - [[AGRICULTURE/Crop-Domestication]]

### ~8,000 BCE
- **Tuber Domestication Begins** - Andes (potato, oca, ulluco)
  - [[AGRICULTURE/Tuber-Agriculture]]
  - [[Asia/Neolithic-Technology]]

### ~7,500 BCE
- **Mehrgarh Phase I** - Early Indus Valley
  - Wheat, barley, mud-brick, early dentistry
  - [[Asia/Neolithic-Technology]]
  - [[MEDICINE/Dental-Care]]
  - [[MEDICINE/Archaeosurgery]]

## Middle Neolithic (~7 ka – 5 ka)

### ~7,000 BCE
- **Linear Pottery Culture (LBK)** - Europe
  - Longhouses, incised pottery, polished axes
  - [[Europe/Neolithic-Technology]]
  - [[Lithic_Technologies/Axe]]
  - [[Lithic_Technologies/Neolithic_Pottery]]

### ~7,000 BCE
- **Cardial Ware** - Mediterranean
  - Impressed pottery, maritime spread
  - [[Europe/Neolithic-Technology]]

### ~6,500 BCE
- **Çatalhöyük** - Large Neolithic settlement
  - Wall paintings, figurines, obsidian trade
  - [[Asia/Neolithic-Technology]]

### ~6,000 BCE
- **Maize Domestication Begins** - Mesoamerica
  - From teosinte in Balsas Valley
  - [[AGRICULTURE/Maize-Agriculture]]
  - [[REGIONAL-MESOAMERICAN/Mesoamerican-Tech]]

### ~5,500 BCE
- **Vinča Culture** - Balkans
  - Early copper metallurgy, proto-writing
  - [[Metallurgy/Copper-Smelting-Sites-and-Techniques]]
  - [[Europe/Neolithic-Technology]]

### ~5,000 BCE
- **Yangshao Culture** - Yellow River
  - Painted pottery, millet agriculture
  - [[Asia/Neolithic-Technology]]
  - [[AGRICULTURE/Crop-Domestication]]

### ~5,000 BCE
- **Quinoa Domestication Begins** - Andes (Lake Titicaca)
  - [[AGRICULTURE/Quinoa-Domestication]]
  - [[REGIONAL-MESOAMERICAN/Aztec-Technology]] (Inca inheritance)

## Late Neolithic / Chalcolithic (~5 ka – 3.3 ka)

### ~5,000 BCE
- **Cucuteni-Trypillia Culture** - Eastern Europe
  - Mega-sites, painted pottery
  - [[Europe/Neolithic-Technology]]

### ~4,800 BCE
- **First Copper Smelting** - Balkans (Ai Bunar, Rudna Glava)
  - [[Metallurgy/Copper-Smelting-Sites-and-Techniques]]

### ~4,000 BCE
- **Uruk Period** - Mesopotamia
  - Urbanization, writing, wheel
  - [[Metallurgy/Bronze-Age-Archaeology]]

### ~3,800 BCE
- **Maize Staple in Mesoamerica** - Nixtamalization
  - [[AGRICULTURE/Maize-Agriculture]]
  - [[REGIONAL-MESOAMERICAN/Mesoamerican-Tech]]

### ~3,500 BCE
- **Wheel Invention** - Mesopotamia/Europe
  - [[Europe/Neolithic-Technology]]
  - [[Metallurgy/Bronze-Age-Archaeology]]

### ~3,300 BCE
- **Ötzi the Iceman** - Copper axe, clothing, medicine
  - [[Metallurgy/Copper-Smelting-Sites-and-Techniques]]
  - [[CLOTHING/Fursuits]]
  - [[MEDICINE/Traditional-Medicine]]
  - [[MEDICINE/Dental-Care]]

- **Egyptian Unification** - Early Dynastic

## Middle Bronze Age (~2.5 ka – 1.6 ka)

### ~2,500 BCE
- **Andronovo Culture** - Steppe chariots
  - Spoked wheel, horse domestication
  - [[Metallurgy/Bronze-Age-Archaeology]]

### ~2,300 BCE
- **Akkadian Empire** - First empire
  - [[Metallurgy/Bronze-Age-Archaeology]]

### ~2,000 BCE
- **Xia Dynasty** - Traditional first Chinese dynasty
  - Bronze ritual vessels
  - [[Asia/Neolithic-Technology]]

### ~2,000 BCE
- **Olmec Civilization** - Mesoamerica
  - Monumental sculpture, rubber, writing
  - [[REGIONAL-MESOAMERICAN/Olmec-Technology]]
  - [[REGIONAL-MESOAMERICAN/Mesoamerican-Tech]]

### ~1,800 BCE
- **Hammurabi's Code** - Babylon
  - [[Metallurgy/Bronze-Age-Archaeology]]

### ~1,600 BCE
- **Shang Dynasty** - Oracle bones, bronze casting
  - Piece-mold casting, ritual bronzes
  - [[Metallurgy/Lost-Wax-Casting]]
  - [[Asia/Neolithic-Technology]]

## Late Bronze Age (~1.6 ka – 1.2 ka)

### ~1,500 BCE
- **Mycenaean Greece** - Linear B, citadels
  - [[Metallurgy/Bronze-Age-Archaeology]]

### ~1,500 BCE
- **Polynesian Expansion Begins** - Lapita culture
  - [[Oceania/Polynesian-Navigation]]

### ~1,300 BCE
- **Hittite Iron Working** - Early iron
  - Transition to Iron Age
  - [[Metallurgy/Bronze-Age-Archaeology]]
