# Primitive Tech Wiki

A knowledge base documenting prehistoric and early historical technologies from the Stone Age through the Bronze Age.

## About

This wiki documents the technological evolution of early human societies, covering:

- **Stone Age technologies** (3.3 million BCE - 3,300 BCE): Paleolithic tools, lithic industries, regional variations
- **Bronze Age metallurgy** (3,300 BCE - 1,200 BCE): Copper smelting, lost-wax casting, trade networks
- **Agriculture & Domestication** (12,000 BCE - 3,300 BCE): Crop domestication, pottery, early farming
- **Regional technologies** across Europe, Asia, Africa, Americas, and Oceania
- **Specialized technologies**: Medicine, clothing, transportation, construction

## Structure

The wiki is organized into the following categories:

- **AGRICULTURE** - Crop domestication and early farming
- **Americas** - Technology across American continents
- **Lithic Technologies** - Stone Age tools and techniques
- **Metallurgy** - Metal working and smelting
- **Oceania** - Technology in Oceanic regions
- **Medicine** - Prehistoric health and healing practices
- **Clothing** - Textiles, furs, and wearable technology
