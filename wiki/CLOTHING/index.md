# Clothing & Textile Technologies
**Period:** Paleolithic to Bronze Age  
**Region(s):** Global  
**Culture(s):** All known prehistoric cultures  
**Time Range:** ~120,000 years ago to 1,200 BCE  
**Confidence:** Variable by topic  
**Evidence Type:** Material, Archaeological, Ethnographic, Experimental

## Overview
Clothing and textile technologies represent some of humanity's earliest and most transformative innovations. From the first hide scraping in the Paleolithic to the complex woolen textiles of the Bronze Age, these technologies enabled human expansion into diverse climates, facilitated social signaling, and became major economic commodities.

## Quick Reference by Period

| Period | Key Developments | Materials | Tools |
|--------|-----------------|-----------|-------|
| Paleolithic | Tailored hide clothing, eyed needles | Animal hides, furs, sinew | Bone/ivory needles, scrapers, awls |
| Mesolithic | Regional diversification, netting | Hides, plant fibers, nettle | Microliths for processing, bone points |
| Neolithic | Flax/wool cultivation, loom weaving | Flax, hemp, wool, bast fibers | Spindle whorls, loom weights, combs |
| Bronze Age | Wool economy, dyes, trade textiles | Wool, linen, silk (late), dyes | Improved looms, bronze needles, shears |

## Key Sites
- **Kostenki/Sungir** (Russia) - ~30,000 BCE, ivory needles, beaded clothing
- **Dolní Věstonice** (Czech) - ~26,000 BCE, textile impressions on clay
- **Çatalhöyük** (Turkey) - ~7,000 BCE, textile tools, wall paintings of clothing
- **Alpine Lake Dwellings** (Switzerland/Germany) - ~4,000 BCE, preserved textiles
- **Hallstatt Salt Mines** (Austria) - ~1,500-300 BCE, exceptional wool textiles
- **Uluburun Shipwreck** (Turkey) - ~1,300 BCE, textile trade cargo
