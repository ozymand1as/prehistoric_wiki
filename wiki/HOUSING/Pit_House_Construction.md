# Pit House Construction

Pit house construction encompasses the techniques for building semi-subterranean dwellings across cultures and millennia. From Upper Paleolithic mammoth bone structures to 19th century Indigenous pit houses, the principles remain consistent.

## Overview

Pit houses exploit the thermal stability of the earth by sinking the living floor below ground level. Construction techniques vary by environment, available materials, and cultural tradition.

## Universal Elements

### 1. Excavation
- **Depth**: 0.5-2.0 m (deeper = more stable temp)
- **Shape**: Circular, oval, rectangular, square
- **Tools**: Digging sticks, stone hoes, baskets, shovels

### 2. Retaining Walls
- **Earth**: Cut face stabilized with plaster
- **Stone**: Dry-stacked or mortared
- **Wood**: Post-and-plank, log cribbing
- **Bone**: Mammoth ribs, mandibles (Paleolithic)

### 3. Roof Structure
- **Posts**: Central (4-6) + peripheral
- **Beams**: Pole rafters, purlins
- **Covering**: Thatch, bark, turf, earth, hides

### 4. Entrance
- **Types**: Tunnel, ladder/hatch, stepped, ramp
- **Orientation**: Away from prevailing wind
- **Features**: Cold trap, door, windbreak

### 5. Ventilation
- **Smoke hole**: Roof apex, adjustable flap
- **Door**: Draft control
- **Subfloor**: Subfloor flues (ondol, kang)

## Regional Variations

| Region | Depth | Wall | Roof | Entrance |
|--------|-------|------|------|----------|
| **Paleolithic Europe** | 0.5-1m | Bone/earth | Hide/turf | Tunnel |
| **Jōmon Japan** | 0.5-1.5m | Earth/plaster | Thatch/bark | Ladder/hatch |
| **Yangshao China** | 0.5-1m | Plastered earth | Thatch | Stepped |
| **Great Plains** | 1-1.5m | Log cribbing | Earth/lodge | Extended entry |
| **Pueblo SW** | 0.5-1m | Stone/adobe | Timber/earth | Ladder/hatch |
| **Arctic** | 0.5-1m | Whalebone/sod | Sod/turf | Cold trap tunnel |

## Construction Sequence

1. **Site selection**: Well-drained, wind-protected
2. **Layout**: String, stakes, geometry
3. **Excavation**: Remove soil, stockpile for berms
4. **Wall preparation**: Shape, stabilize, plaster
4. **Post holes**: Dig, set posts, backfill
5. **Roof frame**: Beams, rafters, purlins
6. **Roof cover**: Thatch, bark, turf layers
7. **Entrance**: Tunnel, steps, door
8. **Floor**: Clay, stone, compacted earth
9. **Hearth**: Central or wall, ventilated

## Thermal Physics

| Feature | Effect |
|---------|--------|
| **Subterranean floor** | Earth temp ~10-15°C year-round |
| **Earth berm walls** | Reduces ΔT by 50-70% |
| **Thick roof (30-50 cm)** | R-value 20-40 |
| **Tunnel entrance** | Cold air trap, no direct draft |
