# Earth Construction

Earth construction encompasses building techniques using raw earth as the primary structural material: mudbrick, adobe, rammed earth (pisé), cob, and compressed earth blocks. It is the most widespread building technology in human history. [1]

## Overview

Earth construction uses locally available soil mixed with water and stabilizers (straw, dung, lime) to create walls, floors, and roofs. It provides excellent thermal mass, low embodied energy, and is accessible to unskilled labor.

## Techniques

### Mudbrick / Adobe
- **Mix**: Clay, silt, sand, straw
- **Form**: Wooden molds, sun-dried
- **Mortar**: Earth slurry
- **History**: Near East ~9,000 BCE, global spread
- **Modern**: Stabilized (cement, lime)

### Rammed Earth (Pisé)
- **Process**: Damp soil compacted in forms
- **Layers**: 10-15 cm lifts, pneumatic/mechanical
- **Forms**: Removable, reusable
- **Strength**: 1-5 MPa (unstabilized)
- **History**: China (Great Wall sections), North Africa, Europe [2]

### Cob
- **Mix**: Clay, sand, straw (high fiber)
- **Method**: Hand-sculpted, no forms
- **Construction**: Monolithic walls, built in lifts [3]
- **Region**: England, West Africa, Americas
- **Sculptural**: Curved, organic forms

### Compressed Earth Blocks (CEB)
- **Machine**: Manual/hydraulic press
- **Stabilization**: 5-10% cement/lime
- **Standardization**: Uniform size, strength
- **Modern**: 1950s+ (CINVA-Ram)

### Wattle and Daub
- **Frame**: Woven wood lattice
- **Infill**: Earth plaster
- **See**: [[HOUSING/Wattle_and_Daub]]

## Soil Requirements

| Component | Ideal Range | Function |
|-----------|-------------|----------|
| **Clay** | 15-30% | Binder |
| **Silt** | 10-25% | Workability |
| **Sand** | 40-60% | Aggregate |
| **Gravel** | 0-15% | Strength (rammed) |
| **Organic** | <2% | Avoid (decomposition) |

## Stabilizers

| Stabilizer | Dosage | Effect |
|------------|--------|--------|
| **Cement** | 5-10% | Strength, water resistance |
| **Lime** | 3-8% | Plasticity, durability |
| **Bitumen** | 2-5% | Waterproofing |
| **Enzymes** | Trace | Soil modification |
| **Traditional** | Straw, dung, oil | Traditional |

## Performance

| Property | Value |
|----------|-------|
| **Compressive strength** | 0.5-5 MPa |
| **Thermal mass** | High (lag 10-12 hrs) |
| **Insulation (R-value)** | ~0.2-0.4 per cm |
| **Fire resistance** | Excellent |
| **Breathability** | High (humidity regulation) |
| **Embodied energy** | Very low |
