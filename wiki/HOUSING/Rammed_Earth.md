# Rammed Earth (Pisé)

Rammed earth (pisé de terre) is a construction technique where damp soil is compacted in layers within removable formwork to create solid, monolithic walls. It is one of the most durable and widespread earth construction methods.

## Overview

Rammed earth has been used from Neolithic China to the Great Wall, North African kasbahs, European farmhouses, and modern sustainable architecture. It produces walls with high thermal mass, excellent durability, and low embodied energy.

## Technique

### Formwork
- **Panels**: Wood, metal, reusable
- **Dimensions**: 3-4 m long, 30-40 cm wide
- **Alignment**: Clamps, ties, spacers
- **Removal**: After each layer sets (1-2 hours)

### Soil Mix
| Component | Range | Function |
|-----------|-------|----------|
| **Gravel** | 15-30% | Strength, drainage |
| **Sand** | 30-50% | Bulk, workability |
| **Silt** | 10-20% | Cohesion |
| **Clay** | 10-20% | Binder |
| **Moisture** | 8-12% | Optimum compaction |
| **Stabilizer** | 5-10% cement/lime | Modern |

### Compaction
- **Traditional**: Wooden tampers (manual)
- **Modern**: Pneumatic, hydraulic rammers
- **Layers**: 10-15 cm loose → 6-10 cm compacted
- **Density**: >95% Proctor optimum

### Curing
- **Time**: 28 days for full strength
- **Protection**: Plastic, damp cloth
- **Stabilized**: Faster strength gain

## Historical Applications

### China (5,000 BCE – Present)
- **Neolithic**: Yangshao, Longshan foundations
- **Great Wall**: Ming sections (rammed earth core)
- **Tulou** (Fujian): 3-5 story circular forts
- **Modern**: Stabilized, seismic reinforcement

### North Africa
- **Kasbahs** (Morocco, Algeria): Multi-story, decorative
- **Ksour**: Fortified villages
- **Decorative**: Geometric patterns in surface

### Europe
- **Spain** (tapial): Medieval, rural housing
- **France** (pisé): Rhône-Alpes, 18th-19th c.
- **Germany**: Lehmbau, 19th c. revival

### Americas
- **Ancestral Pueblo**: Chaco Canyon core-and-veneer
- **Spanish Colonial**: Southwest US missions
- **Modern**: Southwest US, sustainable building

## Structural Performance

| Property | Value |
|----------|-------|
| **Compressive strength** | 1-5 MPa (unstabilized), 5-15 MPa (stabilized) |
| **Tensile strength** | ~0.1 MPa (low) |
| **Thermal conductivity** | 0.8-1.2 W/m·K |
| **Thermal mass** | High (12-hr lag) |
| **Fire resistance** | 4+ hours |
| **Embodied energy** | <0.5 MJ/kg |

## Advantages & Limitations

| Advantage | Limitation |
|-----------|------------|
| Local materials | Formwork required |
| High thermal mass | Low tensile strength |
| Fireproof | Moisture sensitive (unstabilized) |
| Low embodied energy | Formwork labor |
| Breathable | Erosion if unprotected |
