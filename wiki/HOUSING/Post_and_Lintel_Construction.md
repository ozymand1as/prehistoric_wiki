# Post and Lintel Construction

Post and lintel is a fundamental building system using vertical posts supporting horizontal lintels to create openings and support roofs. It is the basis of timber framing and stone architecture worldwide. [1]

## Overview

The post-and-lintel system is one of the oldest structural principles. It appears independently in timber, stone, and composite forms across all continents from the Neolithic onward.

## Structural Principles

### Load Path
- **Posts**: Vertical compression members
- **Lintels**: Horizontal bending members
- **Foundations**: Transfer loads to ground

### Forces
- **Posts**: Pure compression (if centered)
- **Lintels**: Bending (tension bottom, compression top) [2]
- **Joints**: Shear, bearing, withdrawal

## Timber Post-and-Lintel

### Joint Types
| Joint | Description | Period |
|-------|-------------|--------|
| **Mortise & Tenon** | Projection fits into cavity | Neolithic+ |
| **Half-lap** | Halved ends overlap | Neolithic+ |
| **Scarf** | Angled overlapping ends | Bronze Age+ |
| **Dovetail** | Wedged interlocking | Iron Age+ |
| **Pegged** | Wooden pins secure joints | Neolithic+ |

### Frame Types
- **Box frame**: Posts, plates, tie beams (Neolithic longhouses) [3]
- **Cruck frame**: Curved blades from ground to ridge (Medieval Europe)
- **Aisled hall**: Central nave + side aisles (Iron Age+)

## Stone Post-and-Lintel

### Megalithic
- **Dolmens**: Stone table (capstone on uprights)
- **Trilithons**: Two uprights + lintel (Stonehenge)
- **Temples**: Egyptian, Greek, Mycenaean

### Construction
- **Quarrying**: Leveraging, wedges, fire-setting
- **Transport**: Rollers, sledges, water
- **Erection**: Ramps, levers, shear legs

## Composite Systems
- **Stone foundations + timber superstructure** (Neolithic Europe)
- **Stone posts + timber lintels** (Mycenaean)
- **Timber posts + stone lintels** (rare)

## Advantages & Limitations

| Aspect | Advantage | Limitation |
|--------|-----------|------------|
| **Simplicity** | Intuitive, few parts | Limited span (lintel bending) |
| **Materials** | Universal (wood, stone) | Lintel weight limits opening |
| **Openings** | Doors, windows easy | Heavy lintels needed |
| **Repairability** | Replace individual parts | Settlement issues |
