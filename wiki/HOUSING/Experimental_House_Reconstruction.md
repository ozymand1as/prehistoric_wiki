# Experimental House Reconstruction

Experimental house reconstruction builds full-scale replicas of prehistoric dwellings using period-appropriate tools, materials, and techniques. These projects test archaeological hypotheses and generate quantitative data on labor, performance, and durability. [1]

## Overview

Since the 1960s, experimental archaeology has moved beyond tool replication to full-building reconstruction. Major centers in Europe (Butser, Lejre, Archeon, Pfahlbaumuseum) and North America (Pueblo Grande, Meadowcroft) have built and monitored dozens of prehistoric house types.

## Major Experimental Centers

| Center | Location | Focus | Since |
|--------|----------|-------|-------|
| **Butser Ancient Farm** | Hampshire, UK | Iron Age roundhouse, Roman villa | 1972 |
| **Lejre** | Denmark | Stone Age to Viking | 1964 |

| **Pueblo Grande** | Arizona, USA | Hohokam pit houses | 1990s |

## Key Experiments by House Type

### Neolithic Longhouse (LBK)
- **Butser, Archeon, Samara**
- **Labor**: 3,000-5,000 person-hours
- **Materials**: 50-80 oak trees, 2-3 tonnes clay, 5-10 tonnes thatch [2]
- **Lifespan**: 20-40 years (post rot)
- **Thermal**: 10-15°C above external in winter

### Iron Age Roundhouse
- **Butser, Lejre, Castell Henllys**
- **Labor**: 1,500-3,000 person-hours
- **Posts**: 15-25 oak, 0.5 m deep
- **Thatch**: 30-50 cm water reed
- **Smoke management**: Door control, roof vent

### Bronze Age Byre-House
- **Samara, Lejre**
- **Integrated byre**: Cattle heat raises interior 5-8°C [3]
- **Drainage**: Central gully critical
- **Manure management**: Daily labor

### Lake Dwelling (Pfahlbauten)
- **Pfahlbaumuseum, Archeon**
- **Piles**: Driven 2-3 m into lakebed
- **Floor**: 1 m above water
- **Preservation**: Waterlogged = exceptional organics [4]

### Pit House (Jōmon, Great Plains)
- **Samara, Pueblo Grande**
- **Thermal**: 10-20°C above external
- **Ventilation**: Smoke hole + door control
- **Condensation**: Managed by floor drainage

## Key Insights

### Construction
1. **Post charring extends life 2-3x**
2. **Trench foundations faster than individual holes**
3. **Community labor essential** (15+ people)
4. **Seasonal timing**: Fell timber winter, build spring

### Performance
1. **Thermal comfort**: All types maintain 10-20°C above external
2. **Smoke management**: Door control > roof vent
3. **Condensation**: Major issue in pit houses
3. **Durability**: Post rot at ground line = main failure

### Social
1. **Minimum crew**: 10-15 for timber framing
2. **Skill specialization**: Carpenter, thatcher, dauber
3. **Child labor**: Thatch gathering, daub mixing
4. **Seasonal scheduling**: Winter fell, spring build, summer thatch
