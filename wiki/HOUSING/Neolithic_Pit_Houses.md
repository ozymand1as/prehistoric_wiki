# Neolithic Pit Houses

Neolithic pit houses (semi-subterranean dwellings) were a widespread architectural form in East Asia and parts of Europe. By sinking the floor below ground level, these structures achieved superior thermal stability in continental climates. [1]

## Overview

Pit houses combine excavated floors with post-supported superstructures. They appear independently in Japan (Jōmon), China (Yangshao, Majiayao), Korea (Chulmun), and Europe (LBK variants), adapted to cold winters and hot summers.

## Regional Traditions

### Japan (Jōmon, ~5,000–300 BCE)
- **Form**: Circular/oval, 3-8 m diameter
- **Depth**: 0.5-1.5 m below surface
- **Posts**: 4-6 central, peripheral wall posts
- **Roof**: Conical, thatch/bark, central smoke hole [2]
- **Entrance**: Tunnel or ladder through smoke hole
- **Evolution**: Deeper over time, internal hearths → peripheral

### China (Yangshao, ~5,000–3,000 BCE)
- **Form**: Round/square, 3-6 m
- **Depth**: 0.5-1.0 m
- **Walls**: Plastered earth, sometimes wattle
- **Roof**: Thatch on pole frame
- **Hearth**: Central or wall-mounted
- **Storage**: Subfloor pits

### Korea (Chulmun, ~4,000–1,500 BCE)
- **Similar to Japan/China**
- **Ondol precursor**: Subfloor flue heating (later) [3]

### Europe (LBK variants)
- **Rare**: Some LBK sites show shallow pits
- **More common**: Late Neolithic/Chalcolithic
- **Combined**: Pit + post-built superstructure

## Construction

### Excavation
- **Depth**: 0.5-2.0 m
- **Shape**: Circular, oval, square
- **Spoil**: Used for berms, roof insulation

### Superstructure
- **Central posts**: 4-6, support roof ridge
- **Wall posts**: Peripheral, support eaves
- **Roof frame**: Poles, rafters, purlins
- **Covering**: Thatch, bark, turf, earth

### Insulation Features
- **Floor**: Clay plaster, sometimes stone flagging
- **Walls**: Earth berm, plastered
- **Roof**: Thatch + turf + earth (high insulation)
- **Entrance**: Tunnel, baffle, door

## Thermal Performance

| Feature | Benefit |
|---------|---------|
| **Subterranean floor** | Stable temperature (earth ~10-15°C) |
| **Earth berm walls** | Wind protection, thermal mass |
| **Thick roof** | R-value 30+ (thatch+turf+earth) |
| **Tunnel entrance** | Cold trap, draft reduction |
