# Organized Warfare

Organized warfare is the institutionalized, large-scale use of violence by political communities. It emerges with social complexity, requiring logistics, hierarchy, and technology beyond the raid or feud of tribal societies.

## Overview

Warfare evolves from interpersonal violence → feud → raid → organized warfare. The key transitions are: professionalization, logistics, fortification, and state monopoly on violence.

## Evolutionary Stages

### 1. Interpersonal / Feud (Band/Tribe)
- **Actors**: Individuals, kin groups
- **Motive**: Revenge, honor, resources
- **Scale**: Few participants
- **Rules**: Customary, limited

### 2. Raiding (Tribe/Chiefdom)
- **Actors**: Task groups (young men)
- **Motive**: Loot, captives, prestige
- **Scale**: 10-100 warriors
- **Seasonal**: Post-harvest, dry season

### 3. Organized Warfare (Chiefdom/State)
- **Actors**: Standing armies, conscripts
- **Motive**: Territory, tribute, ideology
- **Scale**: Hundreds to thousands
- **Logistics**: Supply trains, camps, engineering

### 4. Imperial Warfare (Empire)
- **Actors**: Professional standing armies
- **Motive**: Expansion, control, resources
- **Scale**: Tens of thousands
- **Logistics**: Depots, roads, fleets, bureaucracy

## Military Organization

### Leadership
| Level | Chiefdom | State |
|-------|----------|-------|
| **Supreme** | Chief/King | Emperor/General |
| **Field** | War leaders | Generals/Strategoi |
| **Unit** | War band leaders | Centurions/Decurions |

### Recruitment
| System | Society |
|--------|---------|
| **Volunteer/War band** | Chiefdom |
| **Conscription (corvée)** | Early State |
| **Standing army (professional)** | Developed State |
| **Mercenaries** | Late State/Empire |

### Logistics
- **Supply trains**: Pack animals, carts, ships
- **Depots**: Fortified storage along routes
- **Engineering**: Roads, bridges, siege works
- **Medical**: Healers, herbalists, field stations

## Technology & Tactics

### Bronze Age Innovations
| Technology | Tactical Impact |
|------------|-----------------|
| **Composite bow** | Range, penetration |
| **Chariot** | Mobile shock, command |
| **Bronze weapons** | Standardized, durable |
| **Body armor** | Survival, elite identity |
| **Fortification** | Defensive depth |

### Formations
- **Chariot squadrons**: Mobile archery, shock
- **Phalanx** (later): Dense spear wall
- **Skirmishers**: Archers, slingers, javeliners
- **Siege**: Rams, towers, undermining, starvation

## Social Impact

### Militarization
- **Elite identity**: Warrior aristocracy
- **Gender**: Male warrior ethos
- **Economy**: Resource extraction for war
- **Ideology**: Heroic, divine sanction

### State Formation
- **Coercion**: Monopoly on violence
- **Territory**: Defined, defended borders
- **Bureaucracy**: Logistics, conscription, supply
- **Ideology**: Divine kingship, holy war
