# Age and Gender Organization

Age and gender are fundamental axes of social differentiation in all human societies. They structure labor, ritual, politics, and kinship across the lifespan. [1]

## Overview

Age and gender are not merely biological categories but culturally constructed statuses with prescribed roles, rights, and obligations. Their organization varies from flexible (foragers) to rigid (states).

## Age Organization

### Age Grades / Sets
- **Age grade**: Status based on age range (e.g., "warrior," "elder") [2]
- **Age set**: Cohort initiated together, progresses together
- **Transitions**: Initiation ceremonies mark boundaries
- **Functions**: Labor, defense, ritual, governance

### Life Stages (Cross-Cultural)
| Stage | Typical Age | Roles |
|-------|-------------|-------|
| **Infant** | 0–3 | Dependent, socialization |
| **Child** | 3–12 | Learning, light labor, play |
| **Adolescent** | 12–18 | Apprenticeship, initiation |
| **Young adult** | 18–30 | Peak labor, warrior, parent |
| **Prime adult** | 30–50 | Leadership, specialized skill |
| **Elder** | 50+ | Authority, ritual, wisdom, care |

### Age Sets (East Africa, Plains N.A.)
- **Formation**: Initiated cohort (circumcision, vision quest) [3]
- **Progression**: Junior warrior → Senior warrior → Junior elder → Senior elder
- **Functions**: Warfare, labor, ritual, governance by cohort

## Gender Organization

### Gender Roles (Cross-Cultural Patterns)
| Domain | Typical Female | Typical Male |
|--------|----------------|--------------|
| **Childcare** | Primary | Secondary/Alloparent |
| **Food processing** | Primary | Secondary |
| **Textiles** | Primary (spin/weave) | Rare |
| **Pottery** | Hand-built | Wheel-thrown |
| **Agriculture** | Planting, weeding, processing | Clearing, plowing, irrigation |
| **Hunting** | Small game, trapping | Large game |
| **Fishing** | Inshore, processing | Offshore, deep |
| **Metalworking** | Rare | Primary |
| **Warfare** | Defensive, ritual | Offensive, raiding |
| **Ritual** | Domestic, life-cycle | Public, political |

### Gender Complementarity
- **Not equality**: Different, valued domains
- **Interdependence**: Neither self-sufficient
- **Ideology**: Cosmic duality (yin/yang, male/female principles) [4]

### Third / Alternative Genders
- **Two-Spirit** (Native North America): Male-bodied, female role
- **Māhū** (Polynesia): Gender liminal, ritual
- **Hijra** (South Asia): Male-bodied, feminine, ritual
- **Fa'afafine** (Samoa): Male-bodied, female role
- **Bissu** (Bugis): Five genders, ritual specialists

## Life Course Rituals

| Transition | Female | Male |
|------------|--------|------|
| **Birth** | Naming, seclusion | Naming, presentation |
| **Puberty** | Menarche seclusion, cutting | Circumcision, scarification, vision quest |
| **Marriage** | Bridewealth/dowry, move | Bridewealth, bride service |
| **First birth** | Seclusion, naming | Naming, feast |
| **Parenthood** | Primary care | Provisioning, protection |
| **Elderhood** | Ritual authority, midwifery | Council, war leadership |
| **Death** | Funeral, ancestor | Funeral, ancestor |
