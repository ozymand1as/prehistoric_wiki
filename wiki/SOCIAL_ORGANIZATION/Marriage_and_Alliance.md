# Marriage and Alliance

Marriage is the primary mechanism for creating and maintaining social alliances between kin groups. It transforms strangers into kin, regulates reproduction, and structures economic and political relations. [1]

## Overview

Marriage is not merely a reproductive union but a social contract between groups. Alliance theory (Lévi-Strauss) argues that the incest taboo forces exogamy, creating a circulation of women (or men) that binds society together.

## Marriage Transactions

### Bridewealth (Brideprice)
- **Direction**: Groom's kin → Bride's kin
- **Function**: Compensates loss of labor/reproductive capacity [2]
- **Valuables**: Livestock, metal, cloth, shells, cash
- **Prevalence**: ~60% of societies (Africa, Oceania, parts of Asia)
- **Effect**: Creates affinal debt, stabilizes marriage

### Dowry
- **Direction**: Bride's kin → Groom/Brides kin
- **Function**: Daughter's inheritance portion, pre-mortem inheritance [3]
- **Valuables**: Land, jewelry, cash, household goods
- **Prevalence**: Europe, India, China, Near East (historical)
- **Effect**: Status competition, hypergamy

### Bride Service
- **Direction**: Groom → Bride's kin (labor)
- **Duration**: Months to years
- **Function**: Proves worth, compensates without wealth [4]
- **Prevalence**: Foragers, horticulturalists

### Gift Exchange
- **Reciprocal**: Both sides give
- **Ongoing**: Continues through marriage
- **Function**: Maintains affinal ties

## Alliance Theory (Lévi-Strauss)

### Elementary Structures
- **Restricted exchange**: Two groups, direct sister exchange
- **Generalized exchange**: Three+ groups, circular (A→B→C→A)
- **Mechanism**: Incest taboo → exogamy → circulation

### Complex Structures
- **Prescriptive**: Rules specify who to marry
- **Cross-cousin marriage**: FZD/MBD (matrilateral), FBD/MZD (patrilateral)
- **Function**: Perpetuates alliance across generations

## Marriage Rules

### Exogamy (Out-marriage)
- **Incest taboo**: Prohibits nuclear family, often clan/lineage
- **Village exogamy**: Marry outside village
- **Tribe endogamy**: Marry within tribe

### Endogamy (In-marriage)
- **Caste/Class**: Marry within status group
- **Religious**: Same faith community
- **Royal**: Dynasties, maintain purity

### Prescriptive Rules
| Type | Prescribes | Example |
|------|------------|---------|
| **Matrilateral cross-cousin** | MBD | Dravidian, Aboriginal Australia |
| **Patrilateral cross-cousin** | FZD | Rare, Kachin |
| **Parallel cousin** | FBD (patrilateral) | Arab, Bedouin |
| **Sororate** | Sister of deceased wife | Hebrew, Nuer |
| **Levirate** | Brother of deceased husband | Hebrew, Mongol, Nuer |

## Residence Rules

| Rule | Post-marital Residence | Associated Descent |
|------|------------------------|-------------------|
| **Patrilocal** | Husband's father's village | Patrilineal |
| **Matrilocal** | Wife's mother's village | Matrilineal |
| **Avunculocal** | Husband's mother's brother | Matrilineal |
| **Neolocal** | New independent household | Cognatic/Industrial |
| **Ambilocal** | Choice of either | Ambilineal |

## Divorce & Remarriage

| System | Divorce | Remarriage |
|--------|---------|------------|
| **Bridewealth** | Difficult (return wealth) | Levy/sororate |
| **Dowry** | Easier (dowry stays) | Less common |
| **Bride service** | Easy (service ends) | Common |
| **No transaction** | Easy | Common |
