# Nomadic Pastoralism

Nomadic pastoralism is a mobile subsistence strategy based on herding domesticated animals across seasonal pastures. It emerged with animal domestication and became a major economic and political force across the Eurasian steppe and beyond.

## Overview

Pastoral nomadism is not aimless wandering but a highly structured adaptation to arid/semi-arid environments where agriculture is marginal. It requires sophisticated knowledge of pasture cycles, animal husbandry, and mobility logistics.

## Subsistence Base

### Animal Species
| Animal | Region | Products |
|--------|--------|----------|
| **Sheep/Goat** | Universal | Meat, milk, wool, hides |
| **Cattle** | Africa, steppe margins | Meat, milk, blood, traction |
| **Horse** | Eurasian steppe | Transport, meat, milk (koumiss), status |
| **Camel** | Arabia, Sahara, Central Asia | Transport, milk, meat, wool |
| **Yak** | Tibetan plateau | Transport, meat, milk, fiber, fuel |
| **Reindeer** | Arctic | Transport, milk, meat, antler |

### Seasonal Mobility (Transhumance)
| Season | Pasture | Activity |
|--------|---------|----------|
| **Spring** | Lowland/river valleys | Lambing, calving, milking |
| **Summer** | Highland/alpine meadows | Grazing, cheese making |
| **Autumn** | Return to lowlands | Breeding, culling |
| **Winter** | Sheltered valleys | Fodder, shelter, minimal movement |

## Social Organization

### Segmentary Lineage
- **Patrilineal clans**: Core social unit
- **Segmentary opposition**: Unite against outsiders
- **Collective responsibility**: Blood feud, defense

### Leadership
- **Khans/Chiefs**: Hereditary + achieved (war, generosity)
- **Councils**: Elders, clan heads
- **Military**: Age-sets, warrior societies

### Gender Roles
- **Men**: Herding, raiding, defense, trade, politics
- **Women**: Dairy processing, textiles, camp management, childcare
- **Complementary**: Both essential, distinct domains

## Economy

### Pastoral Products
- **Subsistence**: Meat, milk, blood, dairy
- **Textiles**: Wool, felt, hair (yurts, clothing, trade)
- **Transport**: Pack animals, riding, carts

### Exchange with Sedentary Societies
- **Pastoral → Sedentary**: Animals, wool, felt, dairy, horses
- **Sedentary → Pastoral**: Grain, textiles, metals, luxury goods, crafted items
- **Symbiosis**: Mutual dependence, seasonal markets

### Trade Networks
- **Silk Road**: Steppe nomads as middlemen, protectors
- **Fur trade**: Northern forest → steppe → sedentary
- **Slave trade**: Raiding, markets

## Political Forms

### Chiefdoms
- **Khan/Chief**: Hereditary + achieved
- **Tribute**: From subordinate clans
- **Raiding**: Wealth, captives, prestige

### Confederations
- **Tribal unions**: Xiongnu, Turkic, Mongol
- **Elective khans**: Kurultai (council)
- **Imperial scale**: Mongol Empire, Türk Khaganate

### State Interactions
- **Tribute**: Sedentary states pay off nomads
- **Frontier markets**: Controlled exchange
- **Military service**: Nomad cavalry for states
- **Conquest**: Nomad dynasties (Yuan, Qing, Mughal, Ottoman)
