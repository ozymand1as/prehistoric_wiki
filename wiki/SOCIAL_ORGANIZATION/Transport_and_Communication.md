# Transport and Communication

Transport and communication technologies—boats, wheels, roads, pack animals, messengers—are the circulatory systems of complex societies. They enable trade, administration, warfare, and cultural exchange across distances.

## Overview

Transport and communication co-evolve with social complexity. As societies grow, the need to move people, goods, and information over distance drives technological innovation and organizational complexity.

## Land Transport

### Human Porterage
- **Universal**: All societies, all periods
- **Load**: 20–30 kg (trained porters 40–50 kg)
- **Distance**: 20–40 km/day
- **Cost**: High (human calories)

### Pack Animals
| Animal | Region | Load | Speed | Period |
|--------|--------|------|-------|--------|
| **Donkey** | Near East, Africa | 50–100 kg | 20–30 km/day | 4,000 BCE+ |
| **Mule** | Mediterranean, Andes | 100–150 kg | 25–35 km/day | 2,000 BCE+ |
| **Llama/Alpaca** | Andes | 30–50 kg | 20 km/day | 3,000 BCE+ |
| **Camel** | Arabia, Sahara, Central Asia | 150–300 kg | 30–40 km/day | 2,000 BCE+ |
| **Ox** | Universal (plow/cart) | 500–1000 kg | 15–20 km/day | 4,000 BCE+ |
| **Horse** | Steppe, Europe, China | Rider + gear | 50–100 km/day | 2,000 BCE+ |

### Wheeled Vehicles
| Vehicle | Region | Period | Draft |
|---------|--------|--------|-------|
| **Solid-wheel cart** | Mesopotamia, Europe | 3,500 BCE+ | Ox |
| **Spoked-wheel chariot** | Steppe, Near East, China | 2,000 BCE+ | Horse |
| **Four-wheeled wagon** | Europe, China | 3,000 BCE+ | Ox/horse |

### Roads & Infrastructure
| Type | Culture | Features |
|------|---------|----------|
| **Footpaths** | Universal | Unimproved |
| **Processional ways** | Egypt, Maya, Inca | Paved, lined |
| **Royal roads** | Persia, Inca, Rome | Engineered, stations |
| **Inca road system** | Inca | 40,000 km, bridges, tambos |

## Water Transport

### Boat Types
| Type | Region | Construction | Capacity |
|------|--------|--------------|----------|
| **Dugout canoe** | Universal (rivers/lakes) | Hollowed log | 2–20 people |
| **Skin boat** (coracle, kayak) | Arctic, Britain, Iraq | Frame + skin | 1–10 people |
| **Reed boat** (caballito, tule) | Peru, Lake Titicaca, Iraq | Bundled reeds | 1–5 people |
| **Plank boat** | Egypt, Scandinavia, Pacific | Sewn/peg planks | 10–100+ people |
| **Sail ship** | Egypt, Phoenicia, Aegean, Pacific | Plank, mortise-tenon | Cargo + crew |

### Maritime Networks
- **Mediterranean**: Minoan, Phoenician, Greek, Roman
- **Indian Ocean**: Indus, Dilmun, East Africa, SE Asia
- **Pacific**: Lapita, Polynesian voyaging canoes
- **Red Sea**: Egyptian, Aksumite, Roman

## Communication Systems

### Messengers
- **Foot runners**: Inca chasqui (relay, 240 km/day), Persian angarium
- **Mounted**: Persian, Mongol, Inca (chasqui on foot)
- **Signal**: Fire, smoke, drums, mirrors, flags
- **Writing**: Tablets, letters, quipu carried by messengers

### Signal Systems
| System | Range | Speed | Culture |
|--------|-------|-------|---------|
| **Fire beacons** | 20–50 km | Minutes | China, Greece, Inca |
| **Smoke signals** | 10–30 km | Minutes | Native American, China |
| **Drums** | 5–15 km | Minutes | Africa, Oceania, Andes |
| **Mirrors (heliograph)** | 50+ km | Seconds | Greece, Persia, Inca |

## Information Flow

### Types of Information
| Type | Content | Carriers |
|------|---------|----------|
| **Administrative** | Tax, census, orders | Scribes, messengers |
| **Commercial** | Prices, orders, contracts | Merchants |
| **Diplomatic** | Treaties, gifts, threats | Envoys |
| **Military** | Orders, intelligence, alerts | Signal, riders |
| **Ritual** | Calendar, festivals, omens | Priests, runners |
| **News** | Events, deaths, births | Travelers, bards |

### Speed Comparison
| Mode | Speed | Range |
|------|-------|-------|
| **Foot messenger** | 40–60 km/day | Unlimited |
| **Mounted messenger** | 80–150 km/day | Unlimited |
| **Relay runners** | 200+ km/day | Unlimited (stations) |
| **Fire/smoke signal** | Line-of-sight | 50 km |
| **Ship (sail)** | 50–150 km/day | Coastal/ocean |
