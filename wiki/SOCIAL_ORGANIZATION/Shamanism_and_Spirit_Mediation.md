# Shamanism and Spirit Mediation

Shamanism is a religious practice involving a practitioner (shaman) who enters altered states of consciousness to interact with the spirit world for healing, divination, and community welfare. It is the most widespread and ancient form of religious specialization. [1]

## Overview

Shamanism is not a unified religion but a set of techniques for accessing non-ordinary reality. It appears in virtually all hunter-gatherer societies and persists alongside institutionalized religions in complex societies.

## Core Features

### The Shaman
- **Call**: Illness, dream, inheritance, spirit encounter [2]
- **Training**: Apprenticeship, isolation, ordeal
- **Tools**: Drum, rattle, costume, mirrors, plants
- **Role**: Healer, diviner, psychopomp, mediator

### Altered States of Consciousness
- **Techniques**: Drumming, dancing, fasting, plant allies, sensory deprivation [3]
- **Journey**: Soul flight to upper/middle/lower worlds
- **Spirits**: Allies, guardians, ancestors, animal powers
- **Return**: Integration, interpretation, action

### Functions
| Function | Description |
|----------|-------------|
| **Healing** | Soul retrieval, extraction, herbal medicine |
| **Divination** | Future, causes of illness, game location |
| **Psychopomp** | Guiding dead souls, ancestral communication |
| **Hunting magic** | Game attraction, animal spirit negotiation |
| **Weather control** | Rain, wind, seasonal regulation |
| **Conflict resolution** | Spirit-mediated reconciliation |

## Cosmology

### Three Worlds
- **Upper World**: Celestial, creator gods, ancestors [4]
- **Middle World**: Ordinary reality, nature spirits
- **Lower World**: Underworld, dead, animal masters

### Axis Mundi
- **World Tree/Pole/Mountain**: Connects worlds
- **Shaman's pole/tree**: Ritual axis for journey
- **Cosmic geography**: Maps spirit territories

## Ritual Techniques

### Drumming
- **Rhythm**: 4-7 beats/second (theta brainwaves)
- **Drum**: Frame drum, skin, symbolic painting
- **Function**: Vehicle for journey, call spirits

### Plant Allies (Entheogens)
- **Ayahuasca** (Amazon): DMT + MAOI
- **Peyote** (North America): Mescaline
- **Fly agaric** (Siberia): Muscimol
- **Tobacco** (Global): Nicotine, purifying
- **Coca** (Andes): Stimulant, divinatory

### Costume & Regalia
- **Animal parts**: Feathers, claws, antlers, skins
- **Metal**: Mirrors, bells, chains (reflect, sound)
- **Masks**: Spirit faces, transformation

## Social Role

### Status
- **Respected/Feared**: Power to heal/harm
- **Marginal**: Lives at village edge, different rules
- **Gender**: Male, female, third gender (berdache, two-spirit)
- **Compensation**: Gifts, prestige, not salary

### Community Relations
- **Client**: Seeks healing, divination
- **Apprentice**: Learns through service
- **Skeptics**: Challenge, test, sometimes persecute

## Archaeological Evidence

| Evidence | Interpretation |
|----------|----------------|
| **Burials with regalia** | Shaman burials (Sungir, Bad Dürrenberg) |
| **Cave art** | Therianthropes, spirit journeys (Lascaux, Chauvet) |
| **Drums/rattles** | Musical instruments in graves |
| **Plant residues** | Entheogen use (peyote, ayahuasca) |
| **Therianthropes** | Human-animal figures in art |
