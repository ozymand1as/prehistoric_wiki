# Writing and Administration

Writing is the administrative technology that enables state bureaucracy, long-distance coordination, and historical record-keeping. Its invention marks the transition from prehistory to history.

## Overview

Writing systems develop in the context of state formation to meet administrative needs: resource tracking, taxation, legal codes, diplomatic correspondence, and royal propaganda. They co-evolve with bureaucratic complexity.

## Writing Systems

| System | Culture | Period | Type | Deciphered |
|--------|---------|--------|------|------------|
| **Cuneiform** | Sumer, Akkad, Hittite | 3,300 BCE+ | Logosyllabic | Yes |
| **Hieroglyphic** | Egypt | 3,200 BCE+ | Logoconsonantal | Yes |
| **Hieratic/Demotic** | Egypt | 2,600 BCE+ | Cursive | Yes |
| **Linear A** | Minoan | 1,800 BCE+ | Logo-syllabic | No |
| **Linear B** | Mycenaean | 1,400 BCE+ | Syllabic | Yes |
| **Indus Script** | Harappan | 2,600 BCE+ | Logo-syllabic | No |
| **Oracle Bone** | Shang China | 1,200 BCE+ | Logographic | Yes |

## Precursors to Writing

### Token System (Near East, 8,000–3,000 BCE)
- **Clay tokens**: Geometric shapes = commodities
- **Bullae**: Clay envelopes with tokens inside
- **Impressions**: Token shapes impressed on envelope
- **Transition**: Impressions → pictographs → cuneiform

### Sealings
- **Cylinder seals**: Rolled on clay = signature/ownership
- **Stamp seals**: Pressed on clay/bulae
- **Administrative**: Authorization, sealing containers

## Administrative Uses

### Resource Management
- **Inventories**: Grain, livestock, textiles, labor
- **Ration lists**: Workers, soldiers, priests
- **Tax records**: Land, poll, trade taxes
- **Tribute accounts**: Provincial, vassal payments

### Legal & Diplomatic
- **Law codes**: Hammurabi, Ur-Nammu, Tang
- **Contracts**: Loans, sales, marriages, adoptions
- **Treaties**: Hittite-Egyptian, vassal treaties
- **Correspondence**: Amarna letters, merchant letters

### Ritual & Propaganda
- **Royal inscriptions**: Victory, building, piety
- **Religious texts**: Rituals, hymns, myths
- **Funerary texts**: Pyramid Texts, Book of the Dead
- **King lists**: Dynastic legitimacy

## Scribal Profession

### Training
- **Edubba** (Sumer): "Tablet house," years of copywork
- **House of Life** (Egypt): Temple scriptorium
- **Curriculum**: Sign lists, literary texts, math, law

### Status
- **Elite**: Literate minority (<1-5%)
- **Tax-exempt**: State-supported
- **Career paths**: Scribe → overseer → governor → vizier

## Administrative Architecture

### Archives
- **Palace archives**: Centralized state records
- **Temple archives**: Economic, ritual, astronomical
- **Private archives**: Merchant, family, legal
- **Organization**: Baskets, jars, shelves, cataloged

### Sealing & Security
- **Bullae**: Sealed documents
- **Seal impressions**: Authorization, chain of custody
- **Witnesses**: Named on documents
