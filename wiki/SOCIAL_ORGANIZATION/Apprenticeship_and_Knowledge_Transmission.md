# Apprenticeship and Knowledge Transmission

Apprenticeship is the primary mechanism for transmitting craft skills, technical knowledge, and cultural values across generations in pre-literate and literate societies alike. [1]

## Overview

Before writing, all technical knowledge was transmitted orally and through practice. Apprenticeship structures this transmission through structured, long-term relationships between master and learner.

## Apprenticeship Models

### Kin-Based (Household)
- **Context**: Independent household production
- **Trainee**: Child/relative (often son/daughter)
- **Duration**: Childhood to adulthood (10-15 years) [2]
- **Method**: Observation → participation → independence
- **Knowledge**: Practical, tacit, family-specific

### Institutional (Attached Specialists)
- **Context**: Palace/temple workshops
- **Trainee**: Recruited or hereditary
- **Duration**: 5-10 years (formal stages)
- **Method**: Graded tasks, supervision, assessment [3]
- **Knowledge**: Standardized, institutional, sometimes literate

### Guild (Later Periods)
- **Context**: Urban craft organizations
- **Stages**: Apprentice → Journeyman → Master
- **Regulation**: Guild rules, examinations
- **Mobility**: Journeyman travels (Walz)

## Stages of Learning

| Stage | Duration | Activities |
|-------|----------|------------|
| **Observation** | 1-3 years | Watch, fetch, clean, prepare |
| **Assistance** | 2-5 years | Simple tasks, tool prep, finishing |
| **Practice** | 3-7 years | Core skills, supervised production |
| **Independence** | 1-3 years | Full production, quality control |
| **Mastery** | 10+ years | Innovation, teaching, design |

## Knowledge Types

### Tacit (Embodied)
- **Motor skills**: Muscle memory, gesture
- **Sensory**: Touch, sound, smell, temperature
- **Judgment**: "Feeling" material readiness
- **Transmission**: Only through practice

### Explicit (Codifiable)
- **Recipes**: Proportions, temperatures, sequences
- **Patterns**: Designs, layouts, proportions
- **Formulas**: Alloy ratios, glaze recipes
- **Transmission**: Oral, written, demonstrated

### Ritual/Secret
- **Mythology**: Origin stories, patron deities
- **Taboos**: Forbidden actions, materials, times
- **Rituals**: Initiation, first firing, first pour
- **Secrecy**: Trade secrets, competitive advantage

## Teaching Methods

| Method | Context | Effectiveness |
|--------|---------|---------------|
| **Demonstration** | All | High (visual/motor) |
| **Guided practice** | All | High (feedback) |
| **Verbal instruction** | Explicit | Medium (tacit hard) |
| **Error correction** | Practice | High (learn from failure) |
| **Peer learning** | Workshop | High (social) |
| **Ritual enactment** | Secret/ritual | High (identity) |

## Archaeological Evidence

| Evidence | Indicates |
|----------|-----------|

| **Workshop layout** | Teaching spaces |
| **Textual records** | Apprenticeship contracts |
