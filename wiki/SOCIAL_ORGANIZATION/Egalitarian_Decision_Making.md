# Egalitarian Decision Making

Egalitarian decision making is the political process of band and tribal societies where authority is situational, leadership is persuasive not coercive, and decisions emerge through consensus rather than hierarchy.

## Overview

Egalitarian decision making is not "no leadership" but "leadership without authority." It relies on social influence, shared values, and the threat of fission (group splitting) to maintain cohesion without coercion.

## Decision-Making Process

### Consensus Building
1. **Discussion**: Open, prolonged, all adults participate
2. **Influential voices**: Elders, skilled hunters, shamans, persuasive speakers
3. **No voting**: Continue until agreement or dissenters withdraw
4. **Implementation**: Voluntary compliance

### Key Principles
- **No coercion**: Cannot force compliance
- **Face-saving**: Avoid public humiliation
- **Time**: Decisions take as long as needed
- **Reversibility**: Decisions can be revisited

## Leadership Roles (Situational)

| Role | Basis | Authority |
|------|-------|-----------|
| **Task leader** | Skill (hunt, heal, make) | Specific task only |
| **Elder** | Age, experience, memory | Advisory, moral |
| **Shaman** | Spiritual access | Ritual, healing |
| **Big man** | Generosity, charisma | Influence, not authority |

## Conflict Resolution

### Social Control Mechanisms
- **Gossip/ridicule**: Primary enforcement
- **Avoidance**: Physical separation (fission)
- **Mediation**: Elders, kin intervene
- **Compensation**: Gift-giving restores relations
- **Execution/expulsion**: Extreme (sorcerers, repeat killers)

### Fission as Ultimate Sanction
- **Threat**: "If you don't like it, leave"
- **Reality**: High cost of leaving (loss of kin, territory)
- **Effect**: Strong incentive to compromise

## Decision Domains

| Domain | Process | Participants |
|--------|---------|--------------|
| **Movement** | Consensus | All adults |
| **Resource use** | Custom + discussion | Resource users |
| **Conflict** | Mediation + compensation | Kin, elders |
| **Marriage** | Family negotiation | Families |
| **War/defense** | Consensus + task leaders | Warriors + elders |
| **Ritual** | Shaman + elders | Community |

## Archaeological Visibility

| Evidence | Interpretation |
|----------|----------------|
| **No elite goods** | Egalitarian distribution |
| **Uniform burials** | No status differentiation |
| **Communal structures** | Collective decision spaces |
| **No palaces/administration** | No centralized authority |
| **Settlement fission** | Egalitarian fissioning |
