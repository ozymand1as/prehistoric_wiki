# Ancestor Veneration

Ancestor veneration is the ritualized honoring of deceased kin, based on the belief that ancestors remain active members of the social group, influencing the living and requiring reciprocal care. [1]

## Overview

Ancestor veneration transforms death from a rupture into a continuity. It transforms the dead from absent to present, creating a living genealogy that anchors identity, legitimizes authority, and structures inheritance.

## Forms of Ancestor Veneration

### Household Ancestors
- **Focus**: Recent dead (parents, grandparents)
- **Location**: House altar, hearth, floor burial
- **Offerings**: Food, drink, incense, daily
- **Function**: Family continuity, protection

### Lineage/Clan Ancestors
- **Focus**: Founding ancestors, apical ancestors
- **Location**: Lineage shrine, ancestral hall, clan cemetery [2]
- **Offerings**: Periodic feasts, sacrifices
- **Function**: Group identity, land claims, political legitimacy

### Royal/State Ancestors
- **Focus**: Dynastic founders, deified rulers
- **Location**: Royal temples, imperial tombs
- **Rituals**: State ceremonies, calendar festivals [3]
- **Function**: Political legitimacy, cosmic order

## Practices

### Burial & Post-Burial
- **Intramural burial**: Under house floor (Neolithic Near East, Çatalhöyük) [4]
- **Secondary burial**: Exhumation, reburial, ossuaries
- **Skull cults**: Plastered skulls (Jericho, Ain Ghazal, Çatalhöyük)
- **Mummification**: Natural (Andes, desert) or artificial (Egypt, Chinchorro)

### Offerings
| Type | Context |
|--------|---------|
| **Food/drink** | Daily at household altar |
| **Incense/candles** | Temple, household shrine |
| **Paper money** | Chinese ghost festivals |
| **Animal sacrifice** | Lineage/royal feasts |
| **Libations** | Pouring wine/water/beer |

### Ritual Calendar
- **Death anniversaries**: Individual commemorations
- **Seasonal festivals**: Qingming (China), Obon (Japan), Día de Muertos (Mexico)
- **Collective**: All Souls, Qingming, Chuseok

## Social Functions

| Function | Mechanism |
|----------|-----------|
| **Legitimacy** | Royal ancestry = right to rule |
| **Land tenure** | Ancestral burial = land claim |
| **Identity** | Shared ancestors = group boundary |
| **Social control** | Ancestors watch, punish norm violations |
| **Continuity** | Past-present-future unity |

## Archaeological Evidence

| Evidence | Interpretation |
|----------|----------------|
| **Intramural burials** | Household ancestors |
| **Plastered skulls** | Skull cult, ancestor display |
| **Ancestral halls** | Lineage ritual space |
| **Royal tombs** | State ancestor cult |
| **Offering vessels** | Ritual equipment |
| **Ancestor portraits** | Visual commemoration |
