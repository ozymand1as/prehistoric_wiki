## Missing Major Technology Categories

### 1. **Pyrotechnology & Fire** — *Critical Gap*
No dedicated category. Fire is foundational to all other technologies.
- **Missing topics:** Fire making (friction, percussion, compression), hearth/fireplace evolution, fuel management, charcoal production, kiln/furnace technology, pyrotechnological temperature control, fire preservation/carrying, ritual fire
- **Related pages:** HEARTH_AND_FLOOR_ANALYSIS (HOUSING) touches on this but not systematic

### 2. **Pottery & Ceramics** — *Critical Gap*
Only "Neolithic_Pottery" in Lithic_Technologies (misplaced). Ceramics deserves own category.
- **Missing topics:** Clay sourcing/preparation, forming techniques (pinch, coil, slab, wheel), firing technologies (open, pit, kiln: updraft/downdraft), surface treatment (slip, burnish, glaze precursors), ceramic typology/chronology, functional morphology (cooking, storage, transport, ritual), ceramic petrology/provenance, early figurines, ceramic technology diffusion

### 3. **Woodworking & Carpentry** — *Critical Gap*
Essential for housing, tools, transport, vessels. Currently scattered.
- **Missing topics:** Tool kits (axes, adzes, chisels, wedges, saws, drills), joinery (mortise-tenon, dovetail, scarf, lap), wood species selection/properties, seasoning/preservation, splitting vs. sawing, bentwood/steam bending, wooden vessel construction, sledges/wheels/axles, architectural carpentry

### 4. **Bone, Antler & Ivory Working** — *Critical Gap*
Major Paleolithic-Upper Paleolithic technology, distinct from lithics.
- **Missing topics:** Raw material procurement/selection, manufacturing techniques (grooving, splintering, grinding, polishing), tool types (points, needles, awls, harpoons, mattocks, batons, figurines), osseous projectile technology, ivory working (mammoth, walrus), antler working (red deer, elk), use-wear/residue analysis, symbolic/modified objects

### 5. **Leatherworking & Hide Processing** — *Partial Coverage*
TANNING_AND_HIDE_PROCESSING exists in CLOTHING but leatherworking as technology is broader.
- **Missing topics:** Skinning/butchery for hides, fleshing/dehairing methods, tanning technologies (brain, vegetable, alum, smoke, fat), currying/finishing, leather tooling (stamping, carving, molding), container/bottle making, shield/armor production, cordage/thong production, waterproofing

### 6. **Transport & Vehicles** — *Major Gap*
Only Polynesian Navigation covers maritime. No land transport.
- **Missing topics:** Human porterage/load carrying, sledges/travois, wheeled vehicles (cart, wagon, chariot), wheel/axle technology, pack animals (llama, dog, cattle, horse), riding equipment (bridle, bit, saddle, stirrup), road/trail engineering, bridges/causeways, portage routes, maritime: rafts, dugouts, plank boats, skin boats, sail technology

### 7. **Food Processing & Storage** — *Major Gap*
AGRICULTURE covers production but not post-harvest.
- **Missing topics:** Grinding/milling (saddle quern, rotary quern, mortar-pestle), fermentation (alcohol, dairy, vegetables), drying/smoking/salting, oil extraction, nut processing, storage facilities (silos, granaries, pits, jars), food preservation in permafrost/bogs, cooking technologies (earth ovens, hearths, pottery boiling, stone boiling), feasting equipment

### 8. **Water Management & Hydraulic Engineering** — *Major Gap*
- **Missing topics:** Well digging, spring capture, qanats/foggaras, irrigation canals, terracing, check dams, reservoirs/cisterns, aqueducts, drainage, raised fields (chinampas, waru waru), hydraulic cement, water lifting devices (shaduf, noria, screw pump), flood control

### 9. **Timekeeping, Astronomy & Calendrics** — *Major Gap*
- **Missing topics:** Solar/lunar observation, horizon calendars, shadow clocks/sundials, water clocks, megalithic alignments (Stonehenge, Nabta Playa, Goseck), seasonal indicators (Pleiades, solstices), ritual calendars, astronomical notation/symbols

### 10. **Writing, Notation & Symbol Systems** — *Major Gap*
Only WRITING_AND_ADMINISTRATION in SOCIAL_ORGANIZATION.
- **Missing topics:** Token systems, clay envelopes/bullae, cylinder seals, stamp seals, pictographic systems, proto-cuneiform, proto-Elamite, Indus script, oracle bones, quipu/khipu, petroglyphs/pictographs as notation, tally sticks, mnemonic devices, memory systems (songlines, method of loci)

### 11. **Music & Sound Instruments** — *Missing Entirely*
- **Missing topics:** Flutes (bone, wood, cane), whistles, bullroarers, rattles, drums (frame, kettle, slit), lithophones, musical bows, conch trumpets, rasps, acoustic archaeology, ritual soundscapes

### 12. **Personal Adornment & Body Modification** — *Partial (in Clothing)*
- **Missing topics:** Bead manufacture (shell, stone, bone, clay, glass/faience), pendants, amulets, labrets, ear spools, tattooing (tools, pigments, needles), scarification, body painting (ochre, manganese), hair styling/ornamentation, dental modification (filing, inlay, ablation), cranial deformation

### 13. **Hunting & Fishing Technologies** — *Partial (Spearhead in Lithics)*

### 14. **Animal Domestication & Management** — *Separate from Agriculture*
- **Missing topics:** Dog domestication, sheep/goat management, cattle husbandry, pig management, camelid herding (llama/alpaca/vicuña/guanaco), horse domestication/riding, reindeer domestication, poultry, beekeeping, dairying technologies, wool/hair harvesting, traction animals, pastoral mobility technologies

### 15. **Glass, Faience & Vitreous Materials** — *Missing Entirely*

### 18. **Rope, Cordage & Netmaking** — *Missing Entirely*
- **Missing topics:** Fiber sources (plant, animal, sinew), spinning/plying techniques, ropewalk technology, knot systems, net construction (knotted, looped, twined), fishing/hunting nets, carrying nets, suspension bridges, rigging for vessels/construction

### 19. **Basketry, Matting & Containerry** — *Missing Entirely*
- **Missing topics:** Weaving techniques (coiling, twining, plaiting, wicker), material preparation (willow, reed, rush, bark, root), container forms (baskets, trays, cradles, hats, shields), matting (floor, wall, roof, sleeping), fish traps/creels, wattle for construction

### 20. **Chemical Technology & Material Processing** — *Missing Entirely*
- **Missing topics:** Adhesives (birch tar, pine resin, bitumen, glue), pigments (ochre, charcoal, manganese, kaolin), mordants (alum, tannin, urine), poisons (arrow, fish), soap/saponification, lime/plaster/gypsum production, cement/concrete precursors, fermentation byproducts
